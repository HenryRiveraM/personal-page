# Favicon de cerezas

Generado con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes y exportado en tamaños compatibles con navegadores de escritorio, iOS y Android.

## Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: website favicon and browser tab icon
Primary request: create a compact icon of exactly two joined cherries with two short green stems and one small green leaf, inspired by an elegant personal portfolio. The cherries should be deep intense crimson red with a restrained glossy highlight.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background with alpha channel.
Style/medium: premium simplified 3D icon, clean silhouette, minimal detail, halfway between a polished app icon illustration and a realistic cherry; not a photograph.
Composition/framing: centered, square, the pair of cherries fills about 88% of the canvas with generous separation from the edges; thick readable shapes that remain recognizable at 16x16 pixels.
Lighting/mood: subtle studio lighting, rich dark red fruit, natural green stems and leaf.
Constraints: exactly two cherries, joined stems, one leaf, no face, no eyes, no mouth, no text, no letters, no border, no container, no circle behind, no background, no shadow outside the silhouette, no glow, no watermark, no tiny detached details.
